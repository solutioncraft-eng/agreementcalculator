# Changelog

Newest first. Rendered in-app under Help → What's new, so keep entries in
plain language a workspace user would understand. Sections are `Added`,
`Changed` and `Fixed`.

## 2026-09-02

### Added
- Settings menu in the top bar collects Pricing, People, Branding, Billing and the Audit log.
- Help menu with this change log and a Support & requests form that files tickets with SolutionCraft.
- Sign in with Google. A new Google address starts workspace setup; an existing one signs straight in.
- Public /privacy and /terms pages.

## 2026-09-01

### Added
- Operator portal can remove accounts and delete tenants, and sorts people by tenant.
- Local end-to-end testing guide committed to the repo.

### Changed
- A new workspace starts with generic offerings rather than one MSP's product names.
- Alternative offerings on a quote are identified by their rate, not their position in the ladder.

### Fixed
- Exported PDFs print a minus sign a client can actually see.

## 2026-08-31

### Added
- COGS items are assigned to offerings explicitly, with an optional parent offering; standalone offerings choose their own COGS items while being edited.

### Changed
- COGS membership is shown per offering instead of as a matrix.
- Approved quotes stamp the approval record on the PDF instead of a client-facing watermark.

### Fixed
- Quotes no longer show an upgrade step or a discount that doesn't apply.

## 2026-08-28

### Added
- Achieved service gross margin is surfaced alongside the pricing card.
- Marketing pages are discoverable and shareable (metadata, sitemap, canonical URLs).

## 2026-08-27

### Added
- Workspaces subscribe with a Stripe subscription; billing lives under Settings → Billing.
- Operator portal gains people, comp and trial controls.
- Locked-out users can reset their own password from the login page.
- A pricing version can carry any number of offerings.

### Changed
- The upgrade step is priced off the headline rate.

## 2026-08-26

### Added
- Multi-tenant workspaces with a super-admin operator portal.
- Landing page, self-serve signup and 14-day trial.
- Agreement Calculator brand style guide applied throughout; app and operator chrome work on phones.
- Tenant accent colour and logo on the app and exported PDFs.
