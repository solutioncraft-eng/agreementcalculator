## What the application does

Agreement Calculator prices managed services agreements for an MSP. Your workspace holds a **COGS catalogue** (the tools you pay for), a set of **offerings** built from those tools, a **bundle discount** table and the **pricing settings** that turn cost into a sell rate. An account manager enters a prospect's environment — users, devices, locations — and gets a per-user agreement rate for every offering, ready to export as a client-facing PDF.

Pricing lives in **published pricing versions**. A published version is frozen, so every quote and PDF can be traced back to exactly the numbers it was priced against. Quotes that stay inside policy export immediately; quotes that bend policy go through **leadership review** first.

## Signing in

1. Open your workspace address (for example `acme.agreementcalculator.com`) or the main sign-in page.
2. Sign in with your email and password, or **Continue with Google** if your workspace has enabled it. Google only proves which email address you own; your access still comes from a membership somebody created for you.
3. If you belong to more than one workspace you will be asked to pick one. You can switch later from the header.

:::note
A first sign-in with a temporary password asks you to set a new one before continuing. If your email is not a member of any workspace you will see *no workspace* — ask your administrator to add you under **Settings → People**.
:::

## Finding your way around

| Menu | What is there | Who uses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Calculator** | Price an agreement, submit for review, export PDFs | Everyone |
| **Quotes** | Quotes you have submitted for review and their status | Everyone |
| **Reviews** | Queue of quotes waiting for a decision | Leaders and administrators |
| **Settings → Pricing** | Pricing versions, offerings, COGS items, bundle discounts | Administrators |
| **Settings → People** | Members and roles | Administrators |
| **Settings → Branding** | Logo, accent colour, PDF footer | Administrators |
| **Settings → Billing** | Trial, subscription and invoices | Administrators |
| **Settings → Audit log** | Who did what, and every PDF export | Administrators |
| **Help** | This guide, what's new, and support | Everyone |

## A typical week

- An **account manager** opens the Calculator, enters an environment, checks the rate and exports the agreement PDF. When a deal needs a sharper price they submit it for review.
- A **leader** works the Reviews queue: approve, recommend changes, or deny, with a note the AM will read.
- An **administrator** keeps the pricing schedule current: when vendor costs change they create a draft version, adjust items and settings, and publish.
